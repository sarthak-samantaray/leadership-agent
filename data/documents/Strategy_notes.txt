Adobe Leadership Agent — RAG POC: Strategy Notes
==================================================
Last updated: POC scaffold. Align retrieval questions with these themes when evaluating answers.

1. North-star outcome
---------------------
- Deliver grounded, executive-style answers that combine internal filings and analyst-style materials with optional web verification.
- Default stance: cite sources (document + page where available); flag uncertainty when context is thin.

2. Product pillars (what “good” looks like)
--------------------------------------------
- Grounding: Answers should trace to retrieved chunks, not generic software-industry boilerplate.
- Leadership lens: Frame insights in terms of priorities, trade-offs, risks, and execution—not only raw numbers.
- Actionability: Where possible, end with implications for decisions (portfolio, roadmap, compliance narrative), not just facts.

3. Retrieval strategy (POC)
----------------------------
- Primary: Vector search over ingested PDFs and text notes in data/documents/.
- Diversity: Use MMR (or equivalent) so one long filing does not dominate all chunks for broad questions.
- Optional rerank: When enabled, cross-encoder reranking should lift the most on-topic passages for the exact user query.
- Web: Use as supplement for recency or external facts; keep internal citations primary when the question is about Adobe disclosures.

4. Content scope for this corpus
---------------------------------
- Annual narrative and 10-K/10-Q: financial performance, segment commentary, risk factors, MD&A themes.
- Quarterly earnings materials: management tone, KPIs, guidance signals, Q&A themes.
- Investor presentations: strategic bets (e.g., Creative Cloud, Document Cloud, Digital Experience), platform and ecosystem.
- These Strategy_notes.txt and Operational_tasks.txt: POC operating model and evaluation focus—useful for smoke tests and guardrail tuning.

5. Risk and compliance posture (POC level)
-------------------------------------------
- Guardrails: Rule-based fast path + LLM classification for off-topic or unsafe requests.
- Do not treat this assistant as legal, tax, or investment advice; strategy here is product positioning, not compliance sign-off.
- For production: add audit logs, retention policy, and explicit data classification for each document source.

6. Evaluation themes for demos
-------------------------------
- Factuality vs filings: Does the answer match revenue, segment names, and risk language from sources?
- Attribution: Are sources referenced in a way a user can verify (page, section, or clear quote)?
- Executive tone: Is the answer concise, structured, and free of hallucinated numbers?

7. Open strategic questions (for iterative RAG tuning)
------------------------------------------------------
- How to weight recency (latest 10-Q) vs completeness (10-K risk section) for mixed questions?
- When should the system refuse to answer vs answer with partial context and explicit caveats?
- What chunk size and overlap best preserve tables and multi-line risk-factor paragraphs for this corpus?
