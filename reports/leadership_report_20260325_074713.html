Question

What were the key risks highlighted in the last quarter?

In Adobe's Q1 FY2026, the key risks highlighted were:

  • AI Substitution Risk: There's a concern about AI potentially disrupting Adobe's traditional businesses, particularly the standalone Stock business, which experienced a greater-than-anticipated decline. This risk is linked to generative AI impacting lower-value asset libraries.
  • Leadership Transition: The transition of the CEO to the role of Chair introduced an element of execution risk, although the CEO will remain involved as Chair.
  • Margin Pressure: Operating margins were under pressure, declining year-over-year, though improving sequentially, partly due to increased AI investments.
  • Execution Risk: The combination of AI disruption and the leadership transition introduced undeniable execution risk.

Despite these risks, Adobe demonstrated strong execution, with record revenue and operating cash flow for Q1 FY2026. The company is also making progress in monetizing AI, with a growing number of AI users and evolving AI products like Firefly.
